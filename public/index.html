Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TAAMARA CO-LIVE SPACES in Nanakramguda.

Is TAAMARA CO-LIVE SPACES for men or women? ▼

Both. TAAMARA offers premium co-living accommodation for men and women in Nanakramguda, Hyderabad — working professionals and students are equally welcome.

What room sharing options are available? ▼

You can choose from 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 sharing rooms. Every room is fully furnished and ventilated, and includes the full set of TAAMARA facilities. Contact us for current availability.

What facilities are included in the stay? ▼

Three times food every day, high-speed WiFi, daily housekeeping, a dedicated study & work table, separate wardrobes, a sitting lounge, fridge & water dispenser, washing machines, self-cooking access and power backup — all included.

Is food provided? ▼

Yes — breakfast, lunch and dinner are served every day. You also get access to a kitchen space for self cooking whenever you prefer to make something yourself.

How safe is the property? ▼

Entry is biometric and controlled, entrances and common areas are covered by 24/7 CCTV cameras, and shared spaces are maintained daily — a secure living environment for both men and women.

Where exactly is TAAMARA located? ▼

Road Number 1A, Nanakramguda, Hyderabad, Telangana — a well-connected address in one of the city's most sought-after neighbourhoods. Use the Get Directions button in our Location section.

What is the rent or pricing? ▼

Pricing depends on the sharing option you choose (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 sharing) and current availability. Contact us for current pricing & availability — call or WhatsApp us and we will share the details right away.

How do I book a room? ▼

Three easy ways: send the enquiry form on this page, call us on +91 7386647564 or +91 9848427564, or message us on WhatsApp. We will confirm availability and guide you through the next steps.
